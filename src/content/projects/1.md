## The idea

We came up with the idea after finding a research study from the University of Wisconsin–Madison about using the LiDAR in newer iPhones to check rooms against accessibility standards. We wanted to aim it at an audience that really needed it, so we thought about dementia patients.

We thought, okay, we have the layout from StructureBuilder, we have all the furniture, etc. What is a problem that dementia patients face? One thing was wandering.

## What went right

I think our UI was extremely good for this project.

## What went wrong

I think our ideas still weren't very connected. It didn't make much sense what we were doing with the audit versus what we were doing with the person moving around the house, and I don't think our UI connected those ideas very well either.
